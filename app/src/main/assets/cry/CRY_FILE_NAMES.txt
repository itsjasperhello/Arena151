When a Pokemon is sent out you hear the ball open, then its cry. Sounds never overlap: if both
sides send out at once, Red goes first. A battle starts only after both opening cries finish,
followed by a 3 - 2 - 1 - GO! countdown. Missing cries are skipped with a short pause.
Cries over 4.5 seconds are cut off. Volume follows Settings > Sound effects volume.
After adding files, push to GitHub to build a new APK.
